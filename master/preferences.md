# CV tailoring preferences and guardrails

Updated: 5 November 2026. These are reusable drafting preferences, not claims of legal work authorization.

## Target roles
- QA Lead, Lead QA Engineer, Senior QA Engineer, QA Engineering Manager and Test Manager.
- Technical Delivery Manager / Delivery Manager when experience matches; **do not rewrite previous QA job titles as formal Delivery Manager roles.**

## Location / work mode
- Remote opportunities with English as a working language, particularly EU/UK time-zone teams.
- Current master CV states: “Remote | Open to EU / UK opportunities.”
- Do not claim EU citizenship, residence, visas or work authorization without explicit confirmation.

## CV style
- Professional, concise, ATS-friendly wording, with a **medium-short Experience section** rather than excessive detail or one-line oversimplification.
- Keep the master CV's chronology and verified employer/project descriptions.
- Tailor the summary, relevant skills and experience bullets to the job description; use terminology naturally, not keyword stuffing.
- Focus on concrete scope, actions and supported results; do not make up metrics or exaggerate ownership.
- Maintain a stable, readable document layout based on the existing master DOCX when generating final deliverables.

## Workflow
1. Read `cv.md` first, then `skills.md`, `achievements.md`, `education.md` and this file.
2. Map the vacancy to supported facts and identify material gaps.
3. Ask targeted clarification questions **only** for material missing/contradictory experience; preserve answers as verified amendments only after user confirmation.
4. Generate a tailored draft and a short claims/gap review.
5. Obtain review before application/submission; never apply on the user's behalf without explicit authorization.

## Boundaries
- No fabricated dates, employers, achievements, tools, language levels or seniority.
- Never convert team metrics into individual implementation results.
- Treat conflicting source statements as open questions, not settled facts.
- Do not add private financial, medical, family or other unrelated personal information to public repository files.
